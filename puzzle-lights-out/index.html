How to Play Lights Out

Objective

Turn off all the lights on the grid!

Game Rules

  • Clicking a light toggles that light and its adjacent lights (up, down, left, right).
  • Your goal is to turn off all lights on the board.
  • The fewer moves you use, the better!

Tips

  • Each click changes multiple lights at once.
  • Think strategically about which lights to click.
  • There's always a solution - keep trying!

Winning

When all lights are off, you win the game! A congratulations message will appear.
